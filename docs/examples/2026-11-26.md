## Models & Products

### Anthropic turns Claude Artifacts into standalone desktop apps for macOS and Windows

Anthropic has launched a beta feature that lets users take whatever they build as a Claude Artifact and install it as a native desktop application on macOS or Windows — with no coding required. These apps can fire notifications, live in the menu bar, tap into connectors, and update themselves automatically. Usage requires an active Claude account and counts against existing plan limits.

Source: [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-turns-claude-artifacts-into-standalone-desktop-apps-for-macos-and-windows) — 2026-11-24

### OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra With 3D Simulation Capabilities

OpenAI says GPT-6.2 Astra can interpret and simulate three-dimensional worlds, converting text prompts into interactive 3D scenes by orchestrating external tools such as CAD software and Blender. The model, part of the GPT-6.2 family that debuted November 17, scored 1,318 Elo on the AA-3D benchmark — ahead of Claude Fable 5.2. Astra is positioned for professional and scientific workflows, while the lighter Sol variant targets everyday tasks.

Source: [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/11/25/openai-launches-gpt-62-astra-with-3d-simulation-capabilities) — 2026-11-25

### AI agents now control part of DeepMind's compute for Gemini experiments, but recursive self-improvement remains distant

Google DeepMind VP of Research Koray Kavukcuoglu said AI agents now command a portion of the lab's compute to design and run their own experiments toward the next Gemini model. He described the direction as convergence on a single general-purpose algorithm, but was careful to stress that full recursive self-improvement remains far off. Notably, he said agents' chain-of-thought is deliberately not rewarded during training so that their intentions stay legible and reviewable.

Source: [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/ai-agents-now-control-part-of-deepminds-compute-for-gemini-experiments-but-recursive-self-improvement-remains-distant) — 2026-11-25

## Market

### Drops for Nvidia, Anthropic and other AI stocks drag the markets back down

AI-linked equities pulled the U.S. market lower as a one-day rally faded, with the S&P 500 dropping 0.5% for its eighth decline in nine sessions. Nvidia slid 5.9% a day after surging 31.5%, CoreWeave fell 11.8%, and GE Vernova lost 4.8% as investors questioned whether AI investments will generate profits sufficient to justify the scale of the data-center buildout.

## Policy & Regulation

### Hochul: AI regulation will be a key topic in State of the State address

New York Governor Kathy Hochul said AI regulation will be a centerpiece of her upcoming State of the State address, speaking as she signed an AI-related bill into law. She committed to clear safety standards, embedding independent experts inside companies, and preserving human control over AI systems. Additional measures on data-center sustainability and transparency in generative-AI training data are still awaiting her signature.

Source: [City & State New York](https://www.cityandstateny.com/policy/2026/11/ai-regulation-will-be-key-topic-state-state-address-hochul-says/417439) — 2026-11-24

### Romney slams US policy on AI and China export controls

Former Senator Mitt Romney criticized how the federal government has handled both AI development and export regulation, arguing the U.S. raced ahead on AI without adequate safeguards while still selling China the chips it needs to compete. His comments landed as the Commerce Department added Megaspeed to its trade entity blacklist over allegations that it facilitated smuggling of Nvidia chips into China.

Source: [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6240189-mitt-romney-criticizes-ai-regulation) — 2026-11-24

### AI Regulatory Priorities Heading into the 120th Congress

With the 120th Congress convening, analysts laid out the leading AI legislative priorities: mandatory and enforceable federal safety standards for frontier models, independent pre-deployment testing and audits, and emergency authority for the government to shut systems down. The agenda closely tracks the bipartisan Beyer-Jacobs AI Safety Framework, which calls for safety standards, incident reporting, and clearer liability for harm caused by AI.

Source: [Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck](https://www.bhfs.com/insight/ai-regulatory-priorities-heading-into-the-120th-congress) — 2026-11-24

## Research

### Even the best AI agents struggle with everyday life

New research based on the ShellBench benchmark — drawing on more than 10 million anonymized real-world AI agent interactions — found that leading models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta failed more than a third of ordinary, everyday tasks. The benchmark was developed with researchers from The OpenClaw Foundation, Parsewave, Hugging Face, Red Hat and UC Berkeley, offering a grounded counterpoint to lab-reported capability gains.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/even-the-best-ai-agents-struggle-with-everyday-life/) — 2026-11-24

### What's going on with the Riemann hypothesis?

A newly reported AI-assisted proof of the Riemann hypothesis appears to hold up, though mathematicians are still working through what exactly it establishes. The result arrives amid a string of AI-enabled breakthroughs in mathematics that have unsettled the field, and reporting suggests the work built on long-standing mathematical insights rather than emerging from nothing.

Source: [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/11/whats-going-on-with-the-riemann-hypothesis/) — 2026-11-24
